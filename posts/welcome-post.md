Welcome to my journey about * *name in progress* *, a Web App Prototype designed for DECO2017 class. We will be covering the development of this app, to show that a thought process has been made prior and while creating it. 

At this point, name is still being decided, since the topic has not been chosen either. But I am a bit interested on cybersecurity, specially in the psychology behind the risks people face everyday on the internet.

During this stage, I began looking understanding what the client, BlaBla Corp is actually asking for, even the things that are not explicitely stated. The brief shows that information and experience are equally important, showing that useful content is not the only thing needed for this new tool we have to create.

At this point, i began narrowing the focus towards a specific community. After some research and checking some forums, it turns out phishing through the phone is the most common threat people come across in their workplaces. Instead of thinking about cybersecurity in general, I was considering non-expert users who have corporate jobs who are exposed to  scams, either by their work email, SMS, QRs on public places. Since the information they hold in their power is important, and probably companies don't want it to be compromised.

After seeing this issue, I started thinking about possible solutions to address this issue. Instead of following fixed methods, I explored different approaches inspired by design toolkits, allowing me to think broadly and consider multiple directions.

Some early ideas are related to creating an interactive platform that helps users recognize online dangers and develop safer digital habits. Maybe create a community with this purpose. 

Also, I would have to consider what is most valuable here. Increasing interactivity may affect performance, but making the security concepts too simple could reduce accuracy and usefulness. Additionally, allowing user-generated content introduces risks related to misinformation, so that would have to be considered as well.

Next week i will speak with my teammate and put in common the general topic as well as the ideas we came up with. Hopefully we will follow a security-awareness focus for this prototype. Until next week.
